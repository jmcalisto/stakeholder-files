The Quiz

THE STAKEHOLDERFILES

Five categories. Five gates. At every gate you choose how hard your next question gets.

1–5

At each gate, pick a level. Level 1 is the warm-up, Level 5 is the hardest question in the file.

8–40

A correct answer is worth 8 points per level: 8 at Level 1, up to 40 at Level 5. A wrong answer scores 0.

200

Clear all five gates at Level 5 for a perfect 200. Then the path moves you on, right or wrong.
